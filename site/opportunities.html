Open tenders and recent awards

Live government tenders you can still bid on, and who recently won contracts, taken directly from official publishers. Every entry links to the original notice. Bid through the publisher, never through this site.

From tender to contract

  1. Pick a tender below and read the full notice on the publisher’s site: eligibility, lots and the exact deadline are only there.
  2. Register as a supplier on that country’s procurement portal (linked on each tender, and all 240 portals are listed).
  3. Classify what you sell: portals ask for CPV or UNSPSC codes. Every listing here already carries UNSPSC codes you can reuse.
  4. Study the winners: the awards table shows which buyers bought what, from whom and for how much, so you can price and partner.
