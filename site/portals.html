Government procurement portals

240 places where governments and international bodies publish tenders, covering 196 countries and territories. Register as a supplier there to bid on military and defence contracts.

Check before you rely on a link. This list is compiled from the sources named below and has not been verified link by link. Entries marked “check address” point at a general government or commercial site rather than a procurement system. The official portal is always the system of record for a government’s tenders. Trading with some countries is restricted by sanctions and export controls: confirm what applies to you before you bid.

Which product codes do portals want?

Many portals ask suppliers to classify what they sell: the US uses NAICS and PSC, the EU and UK use CPV, and UNGM, Australia, New Zealand and Canada use UNSPSC. Every listing in this directory carries UNSPSC codes, so you can reuse them when you register.
