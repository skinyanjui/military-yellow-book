Suppliers

Military Yellow Book is a global directory of military suppliers: uniforms, footwear, field gear, rations, vehicle and aircraft parts, communications, medical supplies, marine equipment and logistics.

Complete firearms, ammunition, explosives and weapon systems are not listed. Weapon parts, aircraft parts and body-armor carriers are sold only by licensed dealers and need export licences and end-user certificates. Prices and lead times are set by each supplier; confirm terms in writing before you pay.
